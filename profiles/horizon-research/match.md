# Horizon research profile

Use this profile for research whose consequence is real but not yet proven: new model architectures, interpretability and scientific understanding of models, theory and forecasting, capability results that suggest where the field is heading, and cross-disciplinary work applying machine learning to other domains.

Typical items include a paper proposing an architecture that would matter if it scaled, an interpretability result that changes how we think models work, or an analysis of trajectories and limits.

Do not use this profile for research that is already directly applicable, that is Practice. Do not use it for incremental benchmark improvements, minor variations on established methods, or papers whose contribution is a small delta on a leaderboard. The bar here is deliberately high because research volume is large and most of it will not matter.
