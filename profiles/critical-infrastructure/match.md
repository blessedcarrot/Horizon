# Critical infrastructure profile

Use this profile where AI meets the systems a society cannot do without: energy and utilities first, and also water, transport, telecommunications, and heavy industrial operations. These settings share a posture. Failure is unacceptable, a regulator has standing, assets last decades, and a change has to be justified before it ships.

Three directions belong here.

**AI applied to the sector:** grid operations and forecasting, asset management and predictive maintenance, outage response, network planning, industrial and OT environments, and deployment inside regulated operators where approval and assurance shape what can ship.

**The sector as a constraint on AI:** data centre power demand, grid interconnection queues, generation and transmission investment driven by compute, siting, cooling, and the political or regulatory response to it.

**Sector-specific regulation and obligation:** rules for automated decisions in essential services, reporting duties, resilience standards, and the approval regimes that decide what an operator may deploy.

Do not use this profile for general enterprise AI with no infrastructure dimension, for climate or energy news with no AI or compute angle, or for the general trustworthiness of AI systems, which belongs to Reliability & Assurance.
