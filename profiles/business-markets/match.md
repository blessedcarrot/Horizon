# Business and markets profile

Use this profile when the primary significance of an item is commercial: funding rounds, acquisitions, partnerships and supply agreements, pricing and licensing changes, compute and hardware supply, market structure, leadership moves at labs or major vendors, and regulation with direct commercial teeth.

Typical items include a vendor changing what it charges or who it depends on, an acquisition that shifts who controls a layer of the stack, a financing arrangement that changes who can afford to build, or a policy decision that alters what a company may ship.

Do not use this profile for research results, engineering techniques, tool releases, or safety findings whose commercial consequence is speculative, those belong to Horizon, Practice, or Guardrails. Do not use it for routine funding announcements with no structural consequence, or for stock-price commentary.
