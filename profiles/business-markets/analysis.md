# Evaluation goal

Judge how much an item changes the commercial landscape for organisations building with or buying AI. The reader is a technology leader who must make build-versus-buy, vendor, and dependency decisions.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Restructuring.** Changes who controls a layer of the stack, who can afford to compete, or what everyone will pay, an acquisition of critical infrastructure, a pricing change that resets the market, a supply constraint with industry-wide reach.
- **7-8: Consequential.** Shifts leverage, dependency, or cost for a identifiable set of organisations. A major vendor partnership, a significant acquisition, a regulatory decision that changes what can be shipped.
- **5-6: Worth knowing.** Real commercial news with contained effects, a funding round, an executive move, an incremental pricing adjustment.
- **3-4: Routine.** Ordinary announcements, undifferentiated funding news, or coverage that adds no information beyond the headline.
- **0-2: Noise.** Promotion, rumour without sourcing, or stock commentary.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward items that name concrete terms: amounts, parties, dates, contractual conditions. Ask who gains leverage and who loses it, and whether the answer changes a decision a buyer or builder would make. Penalise speculation about valuations, and do not treat the size of a number as the size of its consequence.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
