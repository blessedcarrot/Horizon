# Energy and infrastructure profile

Use this profile where AI meets energy, utilities, and critical or regulated infrastructure. Two directions both belong here.

**AI applied to the sector:** grid operations and forecasting, asset management and predictive maintenance, outage and demand response, industrial and OT environments, safety-critical control, and deployment inside regulated operators where approval and assurance shape what can ship.

**The sector as constraint on AI:** data centre power demand, grid interconnection queues, generation and transmission investment driven by compute, siting and cooling, energy pricing effects, and the regulatory or political response to all of it.

Typical items include a utility deploying models in operations, a study of AI's electricity demand, a grid operator's position on interconnection, a regulator's rules for automated decisions in essential services, or a financing arrangement whose real constraint is power rather than chips.

Do not use this profile for general enterprise AI with no infrastructure dimension, for climate or energy news with no AI or compute angle, or for vendor announcements that merely mention sustainability.
