# Guardrails profile

Use this profile for items about whether AI systems can be trusted and operated responsibly: security vulnerabilities and attacks on models or AI infrastructure, alignment and safety research with practical bearing, evaluation integrity and benchmark contamination, misuse and abuse, privacy and data governance, and regulation that imposes real obligations.

Typical items include a demonstrated attack against agent systems, evidence that a widely trusted benchmark measures something other than what people assume, a safety finding with deployment consequences, or a rule that changes what an organisation must do.

Do not use this profile for general research on capabilities, for commercial regulation whose significance is mainly financial, or for speculative risk commentary without new evidence.
