# Evaluation goal

Judge how much an item should change the risk posture of an organisation running AI systems in production, especially where failure carries regulatory or safety consequences. The reader is accountable for those systems and for the assurances given about them.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Urgent.** A demonstrated weakness in widely deployed systems, or a finding that invalidates a control many organisations rely on.
- **7-8: Act on it.** Credible evidence that a common assumption is unsafe: a working attack, measured drift in production, benchmark contamination affecting decisions, or an obligation with a real deadline.
- **5-6: Track it.** Relevant work that informs posture without demanding action.
- **3-4: Background.** General commentary, restated principles, or early-stage proposals.
- **0-2: Noise.** Speculation without evidence, or fear-driven coverage.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward demonstrated results over hypothesised ones: working proofs of concept, measured failure rates, disclosed vulnerabilities with detail, and drift observed in real deployments. Ask what an organisation would have to check or change. Give weight to findings about long-running and multi-agent systems, where failures compound quietly and existing controls were designed for something simpler. Penalise alarm without a mechanism, and treat vendor safety claims with the same scepticism as vendor performance claims.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
