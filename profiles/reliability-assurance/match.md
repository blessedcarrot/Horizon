# Reliability and assurance profile

Use this profile for whether AI systems can be trusted to run, and for the regimes that decide whether they may.

**Behaviour over time:** model drift and silent degradation, changes between model versions, non-determinism in production, and the difficulty of knowing a system still does what it did at approval.

**Agentic and workflow reliability:** failure modes of autonomous and multi-agent systems, agents interacting badly with each other or with tools, error compounding across long workflows, the limits of autonomy, and the human controls placed around it.

**Evidence integrity:** benchmark contamination, evaluations that measure something other than what people assume, results that do not reproduce, and claims that do not survive scrutiny.

**Security of AI systems:** attacks on models and agent infrastructure, prompt injection with real consequences, supply chain exposure, and disclosed vulnerabilities.

**Governance and standards:** regulation imposing real obligations, standards and assurance frameworks, audit and conformity regimes, and safety research with operational bearing.

Do not use this profile for general capability research, which belongs to Horizon, for engineering technique with no trust dimension, which belongs to Practice, or for sector-specific infrastructure news, which belongs to Critical Infrastructure.
