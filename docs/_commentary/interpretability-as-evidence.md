THE FACTS, so you do not have to go and get them.

  A pre-registered study ran 15,840 specifications across seven analytic axes on
  one model (GPT-2 small) and one task. 7,561 produced a claim.

  The EU AI Act Annex IV style statement derived from those claims FLIPPED across
  73.2% of specification pairs (95% CI 0.725 to 0.738).

  Standardising the single most influential choice, the evaluation metric, still

  different circuits, not describing the same one differently.

  Limits the authors state plainly: one model, one task, generalisation untested.
